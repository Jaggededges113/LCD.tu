About This Event

Do you hate your landlord? Want to bring the fight to them? ((Legally)) Tenets United is holding a Landlord Combat day, full of legal rights, advice, snack and a Roleplay Activity at the end. Tenants United is a tenants union, kind of like a labor union. If you have some issues with your landlord, and who doesn't, then you might be interested in our workshop: Landlord Combat. We'll go over what legal rights tenants have, how to get your neighbors involved in your shared struggles, and we'll even have snacks and a tabletop roleplay activity at the end led by one of our organizers. You should learn a lot about what your options are dealing with your landlord.

For Tenants and Organizers!

Tenants United is a long running tenants' union that operates on the southside. We're looking for more people to get into organizing, so we put together Landlord Combat. Landlord Combat is a day long workshop for people who want to get into organizing or who would like a refresher on some of the fine points. You'll come out of it with a better sense of who we are and the work we do, as well as a grasp on the skills a prospective organizer would need to organize their building. We'll cover how to canvass, the legal rights available to tenants, and we'll have a tabletop roleplaying activity at the end led by one of our organizers
